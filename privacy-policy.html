Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Clox timer and countdown application (the “App”) handles information. The App is published by Gopal Reddy (“we”, “us” or “our”).

1. No account required

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in.

2. Information handled by the App

Based on the current version of the App, timer settings, countdown details and information used to display home-screen widgets are stored locally on your Android device. We do not operate an account system or our own server to receive this App data.

The App does not request access to your location or contacts. Android may process technical information as part of providing operating-system features such as notifications, alarms and widgets.

3. Notifications and alarms

If you grant the relevant Android permissions, Clox can schedule alarms and show notifications to alert you when a timer or countdown is due. You can manage notification and alarm permissions through your device settings. Disabling these permissions may affect alert functionality.

4. Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. The current App is not designed to send timer or countdown content to us or to advertising or analytics services. If a future version introduces online features or third-party services, this policy will be updated before or when those features are made available.

5. Data retention and deletion

App information is retained locally until you delete it within the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Android backup settings and device migration features may affect how local app data is retained or transferred.

6. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not require an account, we do not knowingly maintain children's personal profiles.

7. Security

App data is stored on your device. Protecting that data also depends on your device security, screen lock and Android settings. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

8. Hosting of this policy website

This policy is published using GitHub Pages. When you visit this website, GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, for security and service operation, in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement. This website is separate from the Clox Android App.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the App's functionality or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
